Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback about Attunex — email njuiverson@gmail.com and you'll hear back from the developer who makes the app.

Attunex is available on the App Store.

How do I follow a person?

Search for the person's name and tap Follow. Attunex gathers the episodes they appear in across shows, newest first, in one feed.

The person I want isn't there

The catalog grows over time, and searches for people who aren't found yet help decide who to add next. To request someone directly, email njuiverson@gmail.com.

Does it work offline?

Yes — download an episode and it plays offline. Saved transcripts and summaries stay readable once downloaded or generated on a supported device.

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

Listening, downloads, unlimited podcast follows, one profile, and a limited number of active people are free. Version 1.5 includes three active people. An optional Pro subscription unlocks unlimited active people and additional profiles. Transcripts and summaries are free on supported devices. The app shows local monthly and annual prices and any trial terms before you subscribe. No Attunex account is required. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which features work on my iPhone?

Attunex requires iOS 17 or later. Listening, downloads, and guest discovery do not require Apple Intelligence. Generating transcripts requires iOS 26 and supported speech recognition for your device and language. Summaries require iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available. Where generation is unavailable, you can still listen and read show notes or publisher-provided transcripts when available.

How do I follow someone from the website?

Download Attunex from the App Store, then search for the person’s name and tap Follow. A website link or QR code does not follow the person automatically. The QR code on a guest page opens that same page on your iPhone so you can keep their name handy.
