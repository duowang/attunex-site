Privacy Policy

Last updated July 1, 2026

Attunex does not require an account and does not use advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

What's stored on your device

The people and podcasts you follow, your app settings, and any episodes you download are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to any server we operate.

Network requests Attunex makes

To find and play episodes, Attunex sends requests to:

  • Apple's Podcasts directory, to search for and match episodes
  • Podcast RSS feeds directly, to load episode audio and details
  • Wikidata, to suggest names while you're adding a guest
  • GitHub's public user search, as a fallback source for a guest's profile picture

These requests go directly from your device to those services and are not routed through, or logged by, any server we operate.

Changes

If Attunex adds features that change what's collected (for example, optional sync or analytics), this page will be updated first and the change will be reflected in the app's iOS Privacy Nutrition Label.
