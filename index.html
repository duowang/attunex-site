      Before you download

      A few useful answers.

      What’s free, and what does Pro add?

      Listening, downloads, unlimited podcast follows, one profile, and following a limited number of people are free. Version 1.5 includes three active people. Pro unlocks unlimited active people and the ability to create additional profiles.

      Transcripts and summaries are also free on supported devices. Pro is an optional monthly or annual subscription; current local prices and any trial terms appear in the app before you subscribe.

      Which iPhones can run Attunex?

      Attunex requires iOS 17 or later. Core listening, downloads, and guest discovery do not require Apple Intelligence.

      Will my iPhone make transcripts and summaries?

      Generating transcripts requires iOS 26 and supported speech recognition for the device and language. Summaries require iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available.

      Where generation isn’t available, you can still listen and read show notes or publisher-provided transcripts when available. A transcript or summary isn’t guaranteed for every episode.

      Does Attunex find every interview?

      Attunex gathers appearances from public podcast feeds and Apple Podcasts, and its catalog grows with each refresh. Coverage can be incomplete. Each guest page shows when its catalog was last updated.

      Can I listen and read offline?

      Yes. Download audio before going offline. Saved transcripts and summaries remain readable once downloaded or generated.

      Do I need an account?

      No Attunex account is required. iCloud sync uses your Apple account. See our privacy policy for details about search requests and how data is handled.
