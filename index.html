Attunex

Follow the people you actually want to hear — Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Gavin Baker. Attunex surfaces every podcast episode featuring them — across every show, not just the ones you subscribe to — with an on-device transcript and summary for each one.

Follow people, not just shows

Most podcast apps organize the world by show. But the person you want to hear from — a founder, a researcher, an author — turns up as a guest across dozens of different shows, and you never find those episodes. Attunex flips it around: follow a person, and their appearances across every podcast come to you in one feed. Interviews, not passing mentions.

Also a full podcast player

Don't want to leave your shows behind? You don't have to. Attunex is a complete podcast player — subscribe to any show and bring your whole library over with OPML import. Following people is an addition, not a replacement.
